FINAL BASELINE FOR DEVELOPMENT · Version 1.0 · 9 October 2026

Purpose: define the product to be built, how each major function should behave, what counts as acceptance, and what must not be assumed. This is a product specification, not a claim that every feature is already implemented or that all commercial proposals have been approved.

Product positioning: India’s auction intelligence and buyer-support platform — combining multi-source auction discovery, understandable risk information, alerts, professional tools and a path from discovery to due diligence.

0. How to read this document

-   “MUST” means required for the product scope described here. “SHOULD” means expected unless the product owner explicitly defers it. “LATER PHASE” means designed for but not required in the initial launch.

-   This document defines desired product behaviour. It does not certify current production capabilities. Existing implementation must be checked against each requirement.

-   Commercial prices and launch targets are carried from the client’s Launch & Scale Strategy as proposed values, not silently treated as approved prices. Product owner must confirm them before payment configuration or public display.

-   Source access is permission-dependent. No source may be added or scraped where terms, robots.txt, authentication, CAPTCHA, 401/403 or other restrictions prohibit access. No source-specific permission is implied by this document.

-   No production database mutation, bulk import, backfill, migration, destructive cleanup, or deployment without explicit owner approval and a rollback plan.

1. Product objectives and success

1.1 Core objective

Help Indian property buyers and professionals find relevant auction opportunities, understand the available information and visible risks, monitor deadlines, share opportunities with clients, and access qualified support when needed.

1.2 Differentiation

-   Broader, permission-compliant coverage beyond public-sector bank notices alone, including approved NBFC/HFC, ARC, DRT, NCLT/IBBI liquidation and official-liquidator sources where available.

-   Speed and freshness: make source publication and last-checked time visible; target publication-to-listing under 24 hours where source access and processing allow.

-   Practical intelligence: structured auction terms, source-linked evidence, historical rounds, explicit missing-data flags and carefully qualified risk indicators.

-   Usable discovery: search, filters, maps, location pages and saved alerts.

-   Buyer and professional workflow: shareable client cards/reports, partner attribution, team access and optional diligence/support services.

3.2 Auction-round status rules

-   Use one central lifecycle rule for cards, detail pages, counts, filters, alerts and reports. Do not create separate contradictory status logic in components.

-   Supported states: Upcoming, Live, Auction Today, Completed, Cancelled, Postponed, and Undated/Needs Review where the data cannot support a reliable date-based state.

-   Upcoming requires a valid future start. Live applies during a valid auction window. Auction Today is used for a qualifying same-day event according to the approved lifecycle rule. Completed applies after the end time/date. Cancelled and Postponed must not be silently treated as active.

-   Undated rounds are excluded from the trusted active-opportunity headline if neither usable start nor end date exists. They are reported separately for review.

-   End-before-start and partial-date records are flagged for review. Do not invent dates or automatically “repair” source facts.

-   Never overwrite source facts merely to make a status look right. Derived display status may be calculated at read time; status transitions must be tested around date/time boundaries and India Standard Time.

3.3 Property vs round counting

-   Count distinct properties, not auction-round rows, for property cards and headline opportunity counts.

-   A property can have more than one auction round and can appear in active and historical views at different times. Active and completed counts are not necessarily mutually exclusive.

-   General catalogue total includes published properties even if they have no auction round. Active opportunity count excludes properties without a qualifying dated open round.

-   Cancelled and postponed rounds are excluded from active opportunity totals and reported separately.

-   Homepage city active counts use canonical, verified city only. Missing canonical city is a separate data-quality bucket, not silently assigned through fuzzy text matching.

-   City-card count and its corresponding results total must use the same eligibility, canonical location and lifecycle definitions.

4.2 Homepage

-   Hero explains the value proposition: find auctions across approved sources, understand key risks and act on deadlines.

-   Prominent search by keyword/location/PIN code, with filters for property type, budget, institution, auction date and status.

-   Coverage counters: eligible notices live today, institutions tracked, states/UTs covered. Counts must be derived from defined metrics, show an “as of” timestamp and must not be inflated by duplicates or stale/blocked sources.

-   Active opportunity city tiles must show qualifying upcoming/live/today properties only. Each tile links to a page whose result count follows the same rule.

-   Sections for Auction of the Day, recently added opportunities, map discovery, relevant guides, video/media where permitted, professional benefits and pricing.

-   Show source freshness and honest limitations. Never claim “verified title”, “guaranteed profit”, “safe investment” or unsupported below-market savings.

4.3 Search and filters

-   Keyword search across permitted structured fields; canonical city, state, PIN code, locality, institution/source, property type, price range, auction date range, possession type and lifecycle status.

-   Sort by newest published/updated, auction deadline, reserve price low-to-high/high-to-low and relevance. Each sort must be deterministic and paginated.

-   Filters combine predictably and are reflected in shareable URLs. Clear-all and active-filter chips are required.

-   Show result count, applied filters, pagination or load-more with stable ordering, empty state, and helpful reset suggestions.

-   No results must not be “fixed” by silently broadening location or text matching.

4.4 Property cards

-   Show property title/type, canonical location (with uncertainty label if unverified), reserve price when available, auction date/deadline, institution, status, source freshness, media thumbnail if permitted, and visible data-quality/risk indicators.

-   Actions: open details, save/watch, share, compare, set alert. Access-controlled actions prompt sign-in or plan upgrade where relevant.

-   Missing data is labelled “Not available in source” or “Needs confirmation”, never fabricated. Placeholder imagery is used unless media type and usage permission are confirmed.

4.5 Map discovery — required feature

-   Provide a map/list toggle and responsive split view on desktop; on mobile use a map screen with a clear list toggle.

-   Plot only records with usable, sufficiently accurate coordinates. Do not geocode uncertain addresses into falsely precise pins.

-   Pins or clusters show a compact summary and link to the corresponding property detail. Clustering is required at zoomed-out levels.

-   Map filters and list filters remain synchronised; changing map bounds may optionally narrow results, with an explicit “search this area” action.

-   Provide loading, no-results, no-coordinate and map-provider-error states. Listings without coordinates remain discoverable in list results.

-   Use an approved map provider, document costs/quotas/terms and privacy implications, and provide attribution as required. Map-provider selection is an implementation decision requiring owner approval if it adds a paid service.

4.6 City, state and region pages

-   Separate city from larger metro-region aggregation. Mumbai city and Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) are distinct views.

-   Proposed MMR membership in the client discussions is a draft requiring owner validation before it becomes production logic.

-   City pages show active count, all-status catalogue count, relevant filters, map/list discovery and pagination. Labels must state what is counted.

-   Only canonical verified city values contribute to trusted city counts. Show or internally report unassigned/unknown location totals.

-   SEO pages must provide useful unique information and must not create thin, duplicate or misleading pages.

4.7 Property detail page

-   Clear title and location; map when coordinates are reliable; reserve price; auction start/end; EMD; increment; institution; auction type; possession; inspection details; eligibility/process notes where source-supported.

-   Current and prior auction rounds with status, date, reserve-price history where available and provenance for each value.

-   Official notice/source links and permitted documents, with access control by entitlement. Display “source notice” and a last-checked timestamp.

-   Gallery for permitted photographs/video, with media type and source/permission controls. If none is cleared, use a neutral placeholder.

-   Risk indicators with explanation, evidence, confidence/review state and date last reviewed. Missing or unreviewed flags must not imply no risk.

-   Share, save, compare, set alert, download/share client card, and request qualified professional help where available.

-   Disclaimers: information is for research, not a guarantee of title, possession, price, legal outcome or auction success. Buyer must review official notice and perform independent due diligence.

4.8 Compare and share

-   Allow users to compare selected properties across location, reserve price, EMD, dates, institution, possession, known risks, document availability and source freshness.

-   Generate a shareable client card/report with BankAuction.co branding and, for eligible partners, their approved name/logo and contact details.

-   Reports must identify missing/unverified fields, carry generation timestamp and link back to source notices. Avoid representing automated summaries as a legal opinion.

-   Control private notes and team data; public share links must be revocable or expire according to product settings.

5. Auction intelligence and legal risk

5.1 Risk fields

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Risk field                          Expected behaviour
  ----------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Possession                          Represent source-stated physical/symbolic/other/unknown; link evidence; do not infer from vague text without a review label.

  Litigation / case references        Capture only source-supported references; distinguish “mentioned in source” from independent legal verification.

  Society/maintenance dues            Show amount/status only when documented; otherwise “Not disclosed in available source”.

  Tenancy / occupancy                 Record source-stated occupancy/tenancy; unknown is not equivalent to vacant.

  Lis pendens / title concerns        Use as a review flag only when supported by cited evidence or a qualified reviewer.

  Document completeness               Show which notice, terms, annexures and other expected documents are available or missing.

  Data confidence                     Per-field confidence/review state and last review date; retain correction history.
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.2 Risk score policy

Do not ship an opaque “legal score” as if it certifies safety. If a score is used, the owner must approve a transparent rubric, evidence standards, weighting, unknown-data handling, review ownership, disclaimers and correction/appeal workflow. Until approved, use descriptive evidence-backed flags (e.g., “Possession type: symbolic — source stated”) rather than a numerical legal rating.

5.3 Review and correction workflow

-   Reviewer sees source facts, extracted fields, evidence links and differences between source and stored values.

-   Reviewer can mark confirmed, source-stated, uncertain, incorrect or needs legal review.

-   Corrections preserve prior value, actor, timestamp and reason. Critical reserve-price/date errors can be escalated and hidden from promotional feeds until reviewed.

-   Publish a correction/contact path with a target review SLA of 24 hours from the client strategy, subject to staffing.

6. Data sourcing, ingestion and provenance

-   Maintain a source register by institution/source category, state/coverage, terms review, robots/access policy, last successful check, last publication date, source health and known limitations.

-   Before enabling a source, record its legal/terms review and approved collection method. Public accessibility alone does not establish reuse rights for images, documents or substantial text.

-   Never bypass authentication, CAPTCHA, robots.txt, 401/403, anti-bot blocks or source restrictions. Stop when refused and record the refusal.

-   Store original source URL, source identifier, retrieval time, publication time when provided, extraction version, and field-level evidence where possible.

-   Normalize institution names, property type, money/date/time formats and location, but preserve raw source values and never erase provenance.

-   Deduplicate conservatively using source IDs and evidence. Potential duplicates go to review; do not automatically merge materially different auction rounds or delete records based only on similar text.

-   Track source runs and show success, no-change, blocked, partial, failed and stale states distinctly. Retry only according to approved retry policy; do not treat refusal as a transient error.

-   No ingestion of findauction.in. No source expansion is authorised by this specification merely because a source category is listed.

-   Do not ingest or display source media unless type and permission are confirmed. No unrestricted downloading of official documents/media merely to create thumbnails.

7.4 Payments, renewal and billing

-   Payment gateway checkout; server-side order creation and verification; signed webhook validation; idempotency; payment status reconciliation; no entitlement based only on client-side success redirect.

-   UPI AutoPay/card recurring payments only if supported and explicitly approved. Obtain mandate consent, show amount/cadence, handle cancellation and failed renewals clearly.

-   Automatic GST invoice generation with configured legal entity/GST details; credit notes/refunds and invoice numbering according to CA-approved rules.

-   Refund policy shown before payment. Strategy suggests a 7-day refund window, but final policy and statutory treatment require owner/CA/legal approval.

-   On payment failure: preserve truthful status, retry only under documented rules, notify customer, avoid duplicate charges and provide support path.

-   Cancellation, renewal reminder, expiry/grace-period and refund flows are testable and recorded.

8. Alerts, notifications and retention

-   Users can create alerts by city, PIN code, bank/institution, budget, property type and auction date/status. Multiple filters combine predictably.

-   Channels: WhatsApp, email and push notifications as planned; channel availability depends on approved providers, user consent, templates, cost and platform capability.

-   Support immediate alerts for newly eligible notices plus daily/weekly digest. The user controls frequency, channels, quieting/pause and unsubscribe.

-   Only send a notification when the listing meets saved criteria and passes defined eligibility/freshness rules. Deduplicate repeated updates and distinguish new listing from material change.

-   Notification content includes concise property details, deadline, source link and timestamp; do not expose masked premium fields in free-tier notifications.

-   Log queued/sent/delivered/failed where provider reports it; retry transient failures within limits and expose operational metrics.

-   Retention workflows: weekly “your city” digest; inactivity re-engagement after 14 days; day-60 quarterly-to-yearly upgrade offer; renewal reminders. These are proposed campaign rules and must be consent-aware and configurable.

13. Security, privacy and legal safeguards

-   Least-privilege roles, secure session handling, rate limits, input validation, CSRF protection where relevant, secure headers, dependency updates and secret management.

-   Server-side premium entitlements and document access; test cache separation so one user’s entitlements/private data cannot leak to another.

-   Explicit consent and opt-out for communications; retain consent timestamp, source and purpose. Apply applicable Indian privacy, consumer, GST, payment and messaging requirements with qualified review.

-   Minimise personal data. Avoid displaying borrower names or other personal information unless there is a documented lawful need and approved policy.

-   No claims that a property title is verified, that returns are guaranteed, or that the platform replaces official auction notices or independent due diligence.

-   Disclose relevant business relationships/conflicts where applicable. Keep listings neutral and do not preferentially promote institutions because of relationships.

-   Log and respond to data/security incidents; define backup, restore, retention and account deletion processes.

-   Production changes require approved PR, CI result, preview review, explicit owner approval to merge/deploy and post-deployment smoke test.

19. Developer delivery checklist

-   Produce a feature-by-feature implementation matrix mapping each requirement ID to existing code, new work, tests and deployment status.

-   Before coding, provide a concise architecture/data-model impact note and identify any external service costs or credentials needed.

-   Implement one focused branch/PR per coherent feature; do not combine unrelated changes.

-   Provide automated tests and clear manual acceptance steps. Report actual command outputs; distinguish a test not run from a test passed.

-   Use test/sandbox credentials for payments and notifications. Never send real campaigns or charge real customers during testing without explicit approval.

-   No production data changes, imports, backfills, migrations, source additions, plan changes or deployment without explicit written approval.

-   Deployment status must be stated as separate stages: implemented, tested, merged, deployed, production smoke-tested. Never call a feature live based only on a successful build.

-   Provide rollback steps, monitoring signals and owner-facing release notes.

20. Definition of done

-   Every P0 requirement has an implementation status and evidence; no “done” claim without tests or direct verification.

-   Search, filters, map, list, city counts and property detail behave consistently and show honest data limitations.

-   Critical dates/prices/source links are traceable to source evidence; unknowns are explicit.

-   Free/paid permissions are enforced server-side and tested against direct access and caching.

-   Payments, invoices, renewal/cancellation/refund, alerts and support are ready before taking paid subscriptions.

-   No unauthorised source or media is used; blocked access is respected.

-   Security, privacy, backup/rollback, monitoring and owner acceptance gates pass.

-   Product owner signs the release checklist before production deployment.
